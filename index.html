Sales Analytics Tool

Unified sales and advertising analytics for Amazon Sellers.

Service Overview

Sales Analytics Tool is an independent software application designed to help Amazon sellers integrate and analyze their sales performance and advertising data in one place.

  • Unified Sales & Ads Analytics: Combines sales data (via SP-API) and advertising data (via Amazon Ads API) into integrated dashboards, enabling sellers to view revenue, profit, ad spend, and ACOS side by side.
  • Automated Daily Reporting: Automatically retrieves and aggregates data across multiple marketplaces every day, so sellers always have up-to-date insights without manual exports.
  • Profit & Cost Analysis: Tracks sales revenue alongside advertising costs to provide a clear picture of true profitability per product and campaign.

Note: This application is currently under active development and testing prior to public release.

Last Updated: November 2026

We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of all user data accessed through the Amazon Advertising API and SP-API.

With explicit user authorization via Login with Amazon (LwA) OAuth 2.0, our application accesses only the minimum necessary sales and advertising data. We do not collect or store buyer PII.
